1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how the docs-epistle app ("the app") handles information when you use it. We keep data collection to a minimum.

2. Information We Collect

The app does not require you to create an account and does not ask for your name, phone number, or location. Any to-do lists, notes, or preferences you create are stored on your own device.

3. How We Use Information

Information stored on your device is used only to run the app's features, such as showing your to-do lists and drug protocols.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

5. Data Security

Data stored on your device is protected by your device's own security settings. We recommend keeping your device locked and up to date.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is intended for medical professionals and is not directed at children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will show when changes were made.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at [docdective@gmail.com].
