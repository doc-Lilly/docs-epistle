Privacy Policy for Docs Epistle

Last updated: 19 November 2026

What the app stores

Docs Epistle stores the information you enter (for example your name, to-do items, flashcards, thesis text, CME entries, calendar notes and water log) only on your device, in your browser's local storage.

What we collect

We do not collect, transmit or sell your personal data. The app has no accounts, no servers that receive your entries, no advertising and no analytics.

Third-party services

The app loads fonts from Google Fonts. Google may receive standard technical information such as your IP address when the fonts are loaded. Google's own privacy policy applies to that.

Your control

Your data stays on the device where you entered it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's site data in your browser settings, or by uninstalling the app. Data on one device is not shared with another device.

Children

The app is intended for healthcare professionals and students and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
